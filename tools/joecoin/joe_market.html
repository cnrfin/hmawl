Rules as they'd be coded

Day number t = year × 40 + season × 10 + (day − 1), with spring = 0 … winter = 3.
Price = start × (1 + growth)^(t / 40) × swing × jitter × winter dip × pump/dump, rounded to whole G and kept between 5 and 9,999.
Swing is smooth noise over ~12 days and ~4 days; jitter is a fresh hash each day. Both come from hash(seed, day).
Joe's days per season: early = {1, 2, 4, 5}[hash mod 4], late = {6, 9, 10}[hash mod 3]. Van keeps the 3rd and 7th, the postman the 8th.
